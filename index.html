What Blitztext Does

🎤

Speech-to-Text

Press the hotkey, speak, release. The transcribed text is pasted instantly or placed on your clipboard.

⚡

Lightning Fast

Transcription in under a second. Local on Apple Silicon or via OpenAI Cloud — your choice.

🔒

Privacy

With local backend, not a single byte leaves your Mac. API keys encrypted in the Keychain.

🎭

Smart Modes

Normal, Rage (rewrite politely), Emoji (enrich), Yoda, Translator — instant translation into 10 target languages — via hotkey or click.

⌨️

Configurable Hotkeys

Custom keyboard shortcuts for each mode. Press-and-hold: hold = record, release = transcribe.

🌍

German & English

Full interface in DE and EN. Whisper transcribes in 50+ languages.

Three Backends — Your Choice

☁️ OpenAI Cloud
🖥️ Custom Server (whisper.cpp)
🧠 Local (WhisperKit, offline)
